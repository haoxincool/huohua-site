常见问题

我的日记数据在哪里？会上传吗？
全部在你手机本地。开发者没有服务器，收不到任何数据。App 唯一的联网行为是 Apple 的系统服务：天气与地名（需你允许定位）、AI 模型与语音识别语言包的下载、读取 App Store 商店区——都由 Apple 处理，开发者看不到。详见隐私政策第 4 节。

回信为什么慢？什么时候来？
火花的 AI 只在手机锁屏后、尤其是充电时的后台窗口里工作，不会在你使用手机时抢资源，所以回应不是即时的：随手记的回应一般在几小时内送到信箱，写给昨天的回信通常在次日早上。想快一点，锁屏并接上电源最稳；打开 App 时若还没来，稍后再看即可。

模型下载失败或很慢怎么办？
AI 模型约 2.4–3.4 GB，由 Apple 的服务器下发。请连接稳定的 Wi-Fi，并确保手机有足够的剩余空间；下载中断后需要从头开始（系统限制），可以稍后再试。重试入口：信箱 › 右上角设置 › AI 模型 ›「重试下载」，或首页引导卡上的「重试」。

哪些机型支持 AI 回信？
需要 6 GB 内存以上的机型：iPhone 12 Pro / 12 Pro Max、13 Pro / 13 Pro Max、iPhone 14 全系、iPhone 15 系列及之后的机型。iPhone 11、12、12 mini、13、13 mini、SE 等 4 GB 机型可以作纯日记使用，记录、时间线、备份等功能不受影响。

界面语言和热线地区怎么定？
显示语言在 信箱 › 设置 › 显示语言 里三选一：简体中文 / 繁體中文（台灣）/ 繁體中文（香港），界面和火花的回信都跟随这个选择；第一次打开时按手机语言自动选好，英文等其他语言默认简体中文。关怀信息里的心理援助热线按你 Apple 账户所属的 App Store 商店区自动选择，不需要也无法手动选；如果显示的不是你所在的地区，请联系当地的心理援助热线。

如何导出、恢复备份？卸载会怎样？
信箱 › 设置 › 备份与恢复 ›「导出全部日记」会生成一个 zip 备份包（可读的 JSON + 语音、照片、视频原件），通过系统分享面板存到 U 盘、文件 App，或 AirDrop 到电脑。恢复：把 zip 放进「文件」App 点一下解压成文件夹，回到同一页点「从备份恢复」选中那个文件夹；恢复是合并式的，已有记录不会重复。卸载 App 会删除手机上的全部日记数据，无法找回——卸载前请先导出备份。（手机的 iCloud / 电脑整机备份也会包含火花的数据，但换机迁移仍以导出的备份包为准。）

紧急情况怎么办？
火花日记是日记工具，不是求助渠道，也无法替你报警或求助。如果你或身边的人有危险，请立即联系当地紧急服务。如果你正处于难以承受的低谷，可以拨打当地的 24 小时心理援助热线：

FAQ

Where is my journal stored? Is anything uploaded?
Entirely on your device. The developer has no server and receives no data. The app's only network activity is Apple's own system services: weather and place names (if you allow location), downloading the AI model and speech-recognition language packs, and reading your App Store storefront — all handled by Apple and invisible to the developer. See Section 4 of the Privacy Policy.

Why are replies slow? When do they arrive?
Huohua's AI only works in background windows after your phone is locked, and mostly while it is charging, so it never competes with you for the phone. Replies are therefore not instant: a response to a quick note usually reaches your Mailbox within a few hours, and the letter written for yesterday usually arrives the next morning. To speed things up, lock the phone and plug it in. If nothing has arrived when you open the app, check back a little later.

The model download failed or is very slow. What can I do?
The AI model is about 2.4–3.4 GB and is delivered from Apple's servers. Use a stable Wi-Fi connection and make sure the phone has enough free space; an interrupted download has to start over (a system limitation), so it is fine to try again later. To retry: Mailbox › Settings (top right) › AI Model › “Retry download”, or the “Retry” button on the setup card on the home screen.

Which iPhones support AI replies?
Models with 6 GB of memory or more: iPhone 12 Pro / 12 Pro Max, 13 Pro / 13 Pro Max, all iPhone 14 models, and the iPhone 15 series and later. 4 GB models such as iPhone 11, 12, 12 mini, 13, 13 mini, and SE can be used as a plain diary; entries, the timeline, and backups all work as usual.

How are the interface language and the hotline region chosen?
Pick the display language under Mailbox › Settings › Display Language — one of Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), or Traditional Chinese (Hong Kong); the interface and Huohua's letters both follow it. On first launch it is chosen from your phone's language; English and other languages default to Simplified Chinese. The mental-health hotline shown in care messages is chosen automatically from the App Store storefront of your Apple account and cannot be set manually; if it is not the region you live in, please contact your local hotline instead.

How do I export or restore a backup? What happens if I uninstall?
Mailbox › Settings › Backup & Restore › “Export all entries” creates a single zip archive (human-readable JSON plus the original voice, photo, and video files), which you save via the share sheet to a USB drive, the Files app, or AirDrop to a computer. To restore: put the zip in the Files app, tap it once to unzip it into a folder, then on the same page tap “Restore from backup” and choose that folder. Restoring merges — existing entries are not duplicated. Uninstalling the app deletes all diary data on the phone and it cannot be recovered, so export a backup first. (A whole-device iCloud or computer backup also contains Huohua's data, but for moving to a new phone rely on the exported archive.)

What should I do in an emergency?
Huohua Diary is a journaling tool, not a helpline, and it cannot call for help on your behalf. If you or someone near you is in danger, contact your local emergency services immediately. If you are going through an unbearable low, you can call a local 24-hour mental-health hotline:
Hong Kong — Suicide Prevention Services 2382 0000 · Macao — Caritas Life Hope Hotline 2852 5222 · Taiwan — 1925 (Ministry of Health and Welfare) · Singapore — Samaritans of Singapore (SOS) 1767 · Malaysia — Befrienders 03-7627 2929 · Canada — 988 (call or text)
